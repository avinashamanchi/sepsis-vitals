# Pilot risk register

Severity is the harm if the risk happens during a pilot with real patients.
Likelihood is assessed **for the system as it is today** (commit recorded
in PROJECT_REVIEW.md §10). Scale: High / Medium / Low. Owners are roles,
because no names have been assigned. Acceptance criteria say what must be
true before the risk is accepted for a silent-mode pilot.

| # | Risk | Severity | Likelihood today | Mitigation in place | Remaining mitigation | Owner | Acceptance criterion |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Data leakage** (train/test contamination; future information in features) | High | Medium | Patient-level splits; features use only earlier observations (`ts < now`, tested); the evaluation cohort is generated separately; paired patient-cluster bootstrap | Temporal and site-held-out splits on real data; review of label timing against feature timing (R2) | Statistician | Locked analysis plan with a leakage review signed before results are seen |
| 2 | **Dataset shift** (synthetic to real; site to site; over time) | High | **High** (the model is synthetic-only; N53) | Validation status `synthetic-development`; `clinical_use: not-permitted`; PSI drift monitor on inputs | Retrain and validate on representative data (M7); recalibrate; monitor drift during the pilot with stop rules | Clinical lead, statistician | E2 in `pilot_criteria.md` met; drift thresholds set |
| 3 | **Missing vitals or labs** | Medium | High (labs are usually missing) | Missingness indicators; median imputation; `data_quality` warnings list every imputed field, a defaulted age and missing history; validation refuses unknown fields | Decide the minimum data (R6); evaluate performance with labs absent (subgroup in the evaluation report) | Clinical lead | R6 decided; subgroup performance with no labs within the S5 target |
| 4 | **Backend failure** (model, database, audit store) | High | Low | Fail closed: 503 / `risk_unavailable`, no default or stale score, "No risk calculated" in the UI; `/ready` and `/model/status` checks; ECS circuit breaker | Alerting on `recent_inference.success_rate`; on-call owner | Operations lead | Failure drill shows no score is released and the UI never shows low risk for a failure |
| 5 | **Alert fatigue** | High | **High.** On the synthetic cohort the deployed alert rule flags 70% of non-septic patients at least once and 2.5 observations per patient-day, mostly from the rule-based scores | Placeholder policy versioned and recorded with each result; alert sources measured | Operating point and alert routing (R4, R5) set on real data; deduplication or suppression policy | Nursing lead | Silent-mode burden within S3 |
| 6 | **False negatives** | High | High (unknown on real data; on synthetic data the model's output falls with fever and tachycardia) | The model cannot lower the rule-based level; a low output carries "does not rule out sepsis" text | Validated sensitivity at the agreed horizon; review of missed cases | Clinical lead | Sensitivity and missed-case review within the S1/X6 limits |
| 7 | **PHI exposure** | High | Low–Medium | Identifier field encryption; no identifiers in logs (tested); sealed records; Pages manual-only with simulator data | Pseudonymous `patient_id` enforced at the interface; DPIA; retention; log-access controls | Privacy officer | DPIA approved; X2 never triggered in a pre-pilot audit |
| 8 | **Unauthorised access** | High | Medium (MFA not enforced; branch protection not applied) | Authentication everywhere; site isolation on every scoring path; MFA with replay protection; refresh-reuse detection | Enforce MFA for clinical roles; apply branch protection; penetration test; revoke the local credentials | Security lead, project owner | E4 met |
| 9 | **Migration failure** | Medium | Low–Medium | Additive migrations; pre-serve migration script; `/ready` gates traffic on the migration head; upgrade/downgrade tested on PostgreSQL in CI | Rehearse against a production-like snapshot; backup before migrating | Operations lead | Rehearsal recorded |
| 10 | **Incorrect deployment** (wrong image, model or config) | High | Medium | Artifact checksums and feature-schema check before load; versions in every result; `SEPSIS_ENV=production` refuses unsafe defaults; circuit breaker | Signed images or digests pinned in the task definition; deployment checklist sign-off | Operations lead | E5 met |
| 11 | **Overreliance** (output read as a diagnosis or a probability) | High | Medium | Decision-support notice; `clinical_use: not-permitted`; "not a calibrated probability"; attribution marked "not causal"; no invalid confidence interval | Human-factors study (G7); silent mode first; training material | Clinical lead, human factors | G7 met |
| 12 | **Unaudited output** (found in this review) | High | Low (fixed in 765a17c) | Every path records before release; append-only, sealed; tested on PostgreSQL | Periodic `verify_record` audit | Operations lead | X1 never triggered |
| 13 | **Model learned clinically inverted relationships** (N53) | High | Certain for the committed model | Documented; the model is not used for any clinical evaluation | Retrain on real data; add monotonicity or attribution checks to the evaluation gates | Clinical lead, ML lead | A new model passes the attribution probes (fever and tachycardia do not lower the output) |
