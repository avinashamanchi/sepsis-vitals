# No-labs feature ablation

This is synthetic development evidence, not clinical validation.

| Feature set | Features | Best model | Held-out AUROC | Held-out AUPRC |
| --- | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| Full | 58 | GradientBoosting | 0.9158 | 0.7544 |
| No labs | 45 | GradientBoosting | 0.8584 | 0.5958 |

AUROC change (no labs minus full): **-0.0574**.

## Method

Both arms use identical patient-level train, validation, and held-out test splits. The no-labs arm excludes lactate, WBC, procalcitonin, all derived lab trajectories, and every lab-missingness indicator before retraining. Both arms used 20,000 generated patients, seed 42, and the same patient-level split.

## Honest interpretation

On this hand-authored synthetic held-out set, the no-labs model achieved AUROC 0.8584. This result is useful for software-development ablation testing only; it cannot establish performance in a district hospital or any real patient population.

## Limitations

- The cohort is generated by a hand-authored synthetic data process.
- The result is not clinical validation and cannot establish performance in patients.
- External retrospective and prospective validation remain required.
